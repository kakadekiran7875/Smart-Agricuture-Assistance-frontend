Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Smart Agriculture Assistant features and usage.

Smart Agriculture Assistant is a comprehensive, free AI-driven digital platform designed for Indian farmers. It provides scientifically verified crop recommendations, instant leaf disease diagnosis via image upload, real-time APMC mandi prices, weather forecasts, and government scheme guidance in local languages.

You enter your soil's Nitrogen (N) and Phosphorus (P) nutrient values (obtainable from any standard Soil Health Card or local testing lab). Our machine learning algorithm matches these parameters against ideal soil conditions for crops like Rice, Wheat, Maize, Cotton, and Pulses to recommend the highest yielding option.

Simply navigate to the Disease Detection page, click "Browse Image" or take a photo of the affected plant leaf with your camera, and click "Analyze Crop". Our AI computer vision model identifies the disease, confidence rating, causes, and recommended chemical or organic treatments.

Visit the Market Prices section to view real-time commodity prices across agricultural mandis. You can search by crop name (e.g., Soybean, Cotton, Onion, Wheat) and filter by state or district to compare rates.

होय! (Yes). Smart Agriculture Assistant is fully localized in Marathi (मराठी) as well as Hindi and English. Click the language button in the header or the language section on this page to toggle instantly.

You can request an expert call back through our "Contact Expert" consultation form, ask questions via the 24/7 AI Farming Assistant, or call the National Kisan Call Centre toll-free emergency helpline at 1800-180-1551.
