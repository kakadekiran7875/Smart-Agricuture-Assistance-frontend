Disease Identified

Early Blight (अल्टरनेरिया करपा)

Confidence Rating: 94.5%

Severity Level Moderate
Pathogen Type Fungal (Alternaria solani)

Characteristic Symptoms:

Concentric brown-black circular rings (target spots) on older bottom leaves, yellowing of surrounding margins, and premature defoliation.

Recommended Control Measures:

  • Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2 g/L.
  • Prune and destroy severely infected lower leaves away from the plot.
  • Avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation to keep leaf canopies dry.
