🏦 Popular Agricultural Loan Schemes

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Short-term credit for crop production and allied activities

Interest Rate: 4% (with subsidy)

Loan Amount: Up to ₹3 lakhs

PM-KISAN Scheme

Direct income support to farmers

Benefit: ₹6,000 per year in 3 installments

Eligibility: All landholding farmers

Agriculture Infrastructure Fund

Financing for post-harvest management infrastructure

Interest Rate: 3% interest subvention

Loan Amount: Up to ₹2 crores

Dairy Entrepreneurship Development Scheme

Support for dairy farming and processing

Subsidy: Up to 25% of project cost

Loan Amount: Based on project size
